# Acknowledgments

## Inspiration

This project was inspired by the timeless work of the Gang of Four and the evolving landscape of mobile development patterns.

### Key References

- **Gang of Four** — *Design Patterns: Elements of Reusable Object-Oriented Software* (1994)
- **Martin Fowler** — *Patterns of Enterprise Application Architecture*
- **Robert C. Martin** — *Clean Architecture*
- **Eric Freeman & Elisabeth Robson** — *Head First Design Patterns*

### Pattern-Specific Credits

- **VIPER**: Originally described by Jeff Gilbert and Conrad Stoll at Mutual Mobile
- **TCA (The Composable Architecture)**: Created by Brandon Williams and Stephen Celis at Point-Free
- **BLoC**: Introduced by Paolo Soares at Google for Flutter
- **Redux**: Created by Dan Abramov and Andrew Clark
- **Coordinator Pattern**: Introduced by Soroush Khanlou
